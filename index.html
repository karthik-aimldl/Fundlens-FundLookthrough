Step 02 / Column mapping

Connect your investment structure

Map the parent fund, child fund and allocation percentage. The amount and currency fields are optional. We will calculate effective exposure automatically.

Calculation method

Effective allocation is the product of all allocation percentages along each investment path. Monetary exposure is calculated from the top-parent amount when available. Missing amounts remain N/A.
